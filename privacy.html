What this notice must cover

A UK GDPR privacy notice is expected to cover at least the topics below (UK GDPR Articles 13 and 14). Each needs wording for Site Tracker:

  • The purposes of each use and the lawful basis for each, including legitimate interests where relied on.
  • Where data comes from, when it is not collected from the person directly. [For example: added by a colleague or another company on a shared project.]

  • How long each kind of data is kept, or how that period is decided.
  • People's rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, data portability, and how to exercise them.

  • The right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
