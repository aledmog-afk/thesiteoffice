The short version

  • Your lift records, wind log, drafts and settings are kept only on your device, in this browser. LiftWatch has no account, no sign-in and no server that receives them.
  • When you look up a postcode, the postcode is sent to postcodes.io.
  • To get the forecast, the site’s coordinates, rounded to 2 decimal places (about 1 km), are sent to Open-Meteo. Your IP address goes with each request.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking scripts. LiftWatch sets no cookies.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site.

What is stored on your device

All of this stays in the browser you use LiftWatch in. Clearing the site’s data in the browser deletes it. Exported files (JSON, PDF) are saved wherever you choose and are then outside LiftWatch.

What leaves your device

Sent toWhenWhat is sent
postcodes.io (api.postcodes.io)Only when you look up a postcodeThe postcode you typed, plus your IP address and the usual details a browser sends with any web request (such as browser type).
Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com)When the forecast is fetched or refreshedThe site’s latitude and longitude rounded to 2 decimal places (about 1 km), the forecast fields requested, and your IP address and the usual browser request details. Not the postcode, names or records.
Cloudflare (hosting)Whenever the site is loadedYour IP address and the usual browser request details, as for any website.

Nothing else is sent. Records, wind logs, names, notes and checks are never sent by LiftWatch. They leave your device only if you export, print or share a file yourself.

Each of these services handles the data under its own privacy terms: postcodes.io, Open-Meteo and Cloudflare.

Analytics and cookies

LiftWatch includes no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts and sets no cookies.

CONFIG: confirm Cloudflare Web Analytics and any other Cloudflare features that add scripts or cookies are switched off for the Pages project.

Why the data is used

The postcode and coordinates are used only to find the site and fetch its wind forecast. The data on your device is used only to show and record your lift assessments.

How long it is kept

Data on your device stays until you or the browser delete it. LiftWatch does not delete records automatically.

Your rights

You can see, export and delete everything LiftWatch keeps on your device yourself: export from the Records screen, and delete by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.
