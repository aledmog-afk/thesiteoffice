What LiftWatch is

LiftWatch is a pilot decision-support tool. It compares a modelled wind forecast with limits that you enter, for planned crane lifts of flat loads on a mobile crane in the UK, and keeps a record of the decision you make.

  • It is never an authorisation to lift. It supports, and does not replace, the lift plan, the Appointed Person’s judgement, manufacturer data, BS 7121 and on-site wind checks.
  • Forecasts are modelled estimates, not measurements. They do not resolve conditions at the crane, such as wind funnelling between buildings.
  • The crane operator has the final decision whether a lift starts, continues or stops (CPA guidance, section 6.4).
  • The method, its sources and its known limits, including the open items awaiting review by a qualified Appointed Person, are on the method page.
  • The capacity, sling and ground-bearing calculators are switched off until they have been reviewed.

Your responsibilities

  • Check every figure you enter against the lift plan and the crane manufacturer’s data.
  • Use the crane’s anemometer on the day.
  • Keep your own copies of records: they are stored only in your browser and are lost if its data is cleared. Export them regularly.
