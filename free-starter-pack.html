Free Site Forms Starter Pack

Five editable Word forms for everyday site paperwork, free to download and use on your own jobs.

What's in the free pack

The Free Site Starter Pack is five editable Microsoft Word forms taken from The Site Office packs. They cover the records most small contractors need every week on a UK site. Download them, add your company name and logo, fill in the project details and print or share them.

  • Site Diary: a daily record of the work done, labour on site, deliveries, visitors, weather and any delays or instructions, with a site manager sign-off. A good diary is often the best evidence you have if a delay or payment dispute comes up later.
  • Sign In Sheet: a daily register of everyone on site. It helps you know who is on site in an emergency and gives you a record of attendance.
  • Plant Pre-Use Inspection: a weekly check record laid out Monday to Friday, with sections for excavators, dumpers, and MEWPs or telehandlers. Operators record their pre-use checks before starting and note any defects.
  • Toolbox Talk: Working at Height: a 10–15 minute briefing on working at height, with an attendance register so you have a record of who was briefed and when.
  • Daywork Sheet: records labour, plant and materials used on extra or changed work, with an "Authorised By" box for the client's or main contractor's representative to sign.

Who it's for

The pack suits sole traders, small builders and subcontractors who want tidy, consistent site records without starting from a blank page. The forms are plain Word documents, so you can change the wording, add rows or drop sections you don't use. They open in Word, and can also be edited in Google Docs or LibreOffice.

Getting the most from the forms

  • Fill them in on the day. Records written up later are harder to rely on.
  • Keep each form with the project file and use the same project reference on all of them.
  • Get daywork sheets signed as soon as the work is done. See our guide to recording dayworks.
  • Review plant checks weekly and take defective plant out of use until it is repaired.
  • Treat the forms as a starting point: adapt them to your site, your plant and your client's requirements.

When you need more

If the free forms are useful, the paid packs follow the same layout. The Site Management range covers delivery records, concrete pour records, snagging lists, handover checklists and accident and near miss reports. The Health & Safety range includes a library of toolbox talks and trade-specific RAMS templates. Our guides also explain pay less notices and what to record on a concrete pour.
