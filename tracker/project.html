Module Permissions

Choose what each person can do in these modules. The project owner gets Dayworks & Variations automatically (shown below as "Owner — full access"); for everyone else it's off until you switch it on. Everyone with Full access gets Toolbox Talks as a Contributor automatically; picking a role here replaces that. People from another company can be given these too; a grant here adds to whatever their company's access allows.

  • Dayworks & Variations. Viewer can see dayworks and variations. Contributor can also create, edit and submit them, send them to the client for approval, and capture the client's signature to approve or reject them on the spot.
  • Toolbox Talks. Viewer can see talks. Contributor can also run talks and record attendees and signatures. Editor can also change your company's library of talk templates.
